Publications

IEEE · 2025 IEEE 9th International Conference on Software Engineering & Computer Systems (ICSECS)

MonBarta: Deep Dive into Psychological Diagnosis of Mental Health Conversations Using LLM

Saidur Rahman Sujon, Ahmadul Karim Chowdhury, Ashek Seum, Anup Kumar Sutradhar, Faisal Muhammad Shah

MonBarta is a synthetic dataset of 200 clinically validated Bengali mental health conversations. Fine tuning of the PsychAI Analyzer Model (PAM) reached 86.84% accuracy and a 4.34 / 5 Average Human Evaluation Score from expert clinical psychologists, using few shot learning and human in the loop validation.

Elsevier · Natural Language Processing Journal, Volume 7, June 2024, 100075

Harnessing large language models over transformer models for detecting Bengali depressive social media text: A comprehensive study

Ahmadul Karim Chowdhury (first author), Saidur Rahman Sujon, Md. Shirajus Salekin Shafi, Tasin Ahmmad, Sifat Ahmed, Khan Md Hasib, Faisal Muhammad Shah

Led development of the Bengali Social Media Depressive Dataset (BSMDD) and a comparative framework benchmarking deep learning, transformer and large language models. The fine tuned DepGPT (GPT-3.5) reached 97.96% accuracy and an F1-score of 0.9804, outperforming every baseline.
